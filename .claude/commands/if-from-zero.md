# If-From-Zero — design step-back

Take a step back on the work just done, before proceeding.

Answer honestly, no rubber-stamping:

- If you had to start from scratch, knowing what you know now, **would you have designed
  your solution the same way?** If not, what would the better design look like?
- **What would the cost be, right now,** to make those changes? (files touched, regression
  risk, effort)
- **What would the benefits be?** (clarity, robustness, consistency with on-foot's patterns
  and the inviolable seams — e.g. the map-provider and persistence seams — long-term
  maintainability)

End with a clear recommendation: refactor now, later, or leave as-is — and why.
